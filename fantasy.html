Genre: Fantasy Series

Book 1: Keeper of the Lost Cities

Author: Shannon Messenger

Rating: ✮✮✮✮✩ 4.8

Keeper of the Lost Cities is a fast-paced, magical adventure starring Sophie Foster, a twelve-year-old girl who has always felt out of place because she can read minds, until she discovers she is actually an elf from a hidden world. The world-building is super fun, the magic system is creative, and the friendships between Sophie and her new classmates feel very real. It is an easy, exciting read that is hard to put down, especially if you enjoy fantasy series.

Book 2: Percy Jackson and the Olympians

Author: Rick Riordan

Rating: ✮✮✮✮✩ 4.6

Percy Jackson and the Olympians is a hilarious adventure that brings Greek mythology into the modern world. Across five books, Percy Jackson grows from a confused twelve-year-old who just learned he is the demigod son of Poseidon into a brave hero fighting to save Mount Olympus. With unforgettable friendships, epic battles against ancient monsters, and Percy's nonstop sarcastic humor, the whole series is impossible to put down from the first page to the final showdown.

Book 3: The Folk of the Air (The Cruel Prince)

Author: Holly Black

Rating: ✮✮✮✩✩ 3.9

The Folk of the Air is a intense, action-packed series that brings a mythological world that relates to modern-day experiences. Jude Duarte, a mortal girl, is brought to Elfame with her twin sister Taryn and her older sister Vivienne. Jude goes from a scared little mortal girl to a intimidating queen. With extreme levels of battling, heart-breaking betrayals and enemies to lovers romance, this series will show you the true power of a girl's feriositiy and the way she fights her battles in hand-to-hand combat.
