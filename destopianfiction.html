Genre: Dystopian Fiction

Book 1: The Hunger Games

Author: Suzanne Collins

Rating: ✮✮✮✮✩ 4.8

The Hunger Games is a gripping dystopian novel set in a future where the government controls society through fear and inequality. The story follows Katniss Everdeen, who volunteers to take her younger sister's place in a televised fight for survival. The suspense, unexpected twists, and difficult choices make the story incredibly engaging. It is a thought-provoking read, especially for anyone who enjoys action, rebellion, and darker stories.

Book 2: The Maze Runner

Author: James Dashner

Rating: ✮✮✮✮✩ 4.7

The Maze Runner is a suspenseful science fiction novel that follows Thomas, a teenage boy who wakes up in a mysterious place called the Glade with no memories of his past. Surrounded by a massive maze filled with dangerous secrets, he and the other boys must work together to find a way out. The constant mysteries, unexpected discoveries, and fast-paced plot make the book incredibly entertaining. It is a great read for anyone who enjoys adventure, suspense, and stories full of unanswered questions.

Book 3: Shatter Me

Author: Tahereh Mafi

Rating: ✮✮✮✮✩ 4.0

Shatter Me is a captivating dystopian novel that follows Juliette Ferrars, a seventeen-year-old girl whose touch has the power to kill. Locked away because of her dangerous ability, Juliette struggles to understand herself while living in a world controlled by a ruthless government. The unique writing style, complicated characters, and unexpected twists make the story stand out. It is an interesting read for anyone who enjoys dystopian fiction, romance, and stories about extraordinary abilities.
